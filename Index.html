ORIGIN STORY

The Day Hyunsuk Brought Her Home

Black suit. Eyes red. Hands shaking. And behind him, small, quiet, clutching a duffel bag: Ruby. 14. Braids. School uniform.

"She can't stay here," Jihoon said. Flat. "We have enemies. We have blood on the floors."

"She has no one else," Hyunsuk said.

"I don't need a room," Ruby whispered. "I can sleep on the couch. I know how to be quiet."

Jihoon crouched. Put the knife down. Flat on the ground. Away from her.

"You don't cook. You don't clean. You go to school. You're a kid."

That night he gave her warm milk with honey. "Because I had a sister too. Once."

5 years later, she still keeps the chipped mug that says TREASURE.

TREASURE RULES

Rule #0: He put the knife down. She stayed.

Before there was a medic. Before there was a family. There was a boy with a knife, and a girl who was scared.

And that's how Treasure began.
